# Design direction

**SUBJECT** A nationwide mobile-kitchen rental site for institutional and commercial teams that need continuity during planned work or emergencies.

**DIRECTION** Industrial food-service field manual with editorial precision.

**PALETTE** Deep navy `#102A36` for authority; stainless `#E8EEF0` for equipment surfaces; paper white `#F8FAF9` for clarity; safety orange `#D85B2B` for primary actions; cold teal `#167D86` for logistics and refrigeration cues; graphite `#26383F` for body text.

**TYPE** Barlow Condensed for authored display headlines, Manrope for body copy, and Barlow for technical labels. Large display headings use an approximately 1.25 modular ratio.

**LAYOUT** Mobile-first asymmetric editorial grid with equipment imagery and operational annotations.

```text
[utility status / quote action]
[brand + inventory + states + planning]
[H1 and 3-sentence offer] [equipment image plate]
[kitchen -> warewashing -> cold storage -> delivery]
[focused inventory table / image plates]
[state directory and map]
[planning process + FAQ]
[quote close + footer]
```

**SIGNATURE** A visible kitchen-operations route line connecting cooking, warewashing, cold storage, and coordinated delivery.

**OMITTED** Decorative gradients, glass panels, generic icon-card trios, and unverified numeric equipment specifications.

Distinctive traits: annotated equipment plates, technical label typography, and the operational route line.
