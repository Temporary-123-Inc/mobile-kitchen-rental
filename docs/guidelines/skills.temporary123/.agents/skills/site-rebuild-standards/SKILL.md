Standards from the team manager for the H1 and opening paragraph of every rebuilt page. Follow them on every page; vary wording and keyword order naturally between pages.

Reference: [DA thresholds doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1hbSGUss8wv5tt88wBEEtBGmR3sUFX-wm4MHANew7MEQ/edit?tab=t.0)

- [KEYWORDS.md](KEYWORDS.md): the Super 9 keyword categories. Read it before choosing H1 keyword phrases.
- [PAGE-DATA.md](PAGE-DATA.md): per-page pricing, delivery, hours, setup time, local content, and the 250-page site build. Read it before building any location page.

These standards apply to the rendered page, whatever framework, language, or folder structure the site uses.

## H1

- State name first.
- At least 30 characters.
- Describe the rental services clearly.
- Vary wording and keyword order between pages.
- **Remote** leads camp keywords: every H1 that names a camp says "Remote" (Remote Man Camp, Remote Base Camp). About 80% of the time people search "remote", not the generic term. Use "Temporary" or "Emergency" in the same spirit.

### Scope by DA

Pick the H1 scope from the page's domain authority (internal content guideline):

| DA | H1 scope |
| --- | --- |
| Over 25 | 4–9 relevant keyword phrases (from the Super 9 in [KEYWORDS.md](KEYWORDS.md)) across service families |
| 20–25 | One focused service family (default) |
| Below 20 | One service family |

### Service families

- **Kitchen Family:** mobile kitchen rentals, dishwashing trailers, commercial refrigeration trailers, walk-in coolers, freezers, refrigerated containers.
- **Remote Man Camp Family:** shower trailers, shower and restroom combinations, laundry trailers, bunk bed sleeper trailers, remote man camp services.

## Opening paragraph

Directly below the H1, 70–120 words. Cover, in order:

1. The services named in the H1.
2. Emergency and planned uses.
3. Short-term and long-term rentals.
4. Customer types.
5. Delivery coordination.
6. Close with an invitation to request availability or a quote.

## Samples

Match the structure of these; change the state, wording, and keyword order per page.

## Verify before finishing

Check the rendered page (browser, or the built HTML), not the source. For each page:

- [ ] H1 starts with the state name and is at least 30 characters.
- [ ] A camp H1 contains "Remote".
- [ ] H1 scope matches the site's DA (one family at or below 25; 4–9 keyword phrases above 25).
- [ ] The 70–120-word paragraph sits directly below the H1 and ends with an availability or quote invitation.
- [ ] The page states pricing, delivery time, rental terms, hours, GPS availability, and setup time ([PAGE-DATA.md](PAGE-DATA.md)).
- [ ] Customers are commercial or industrial, never residential.
- [ ] 5–10 nearby cities and one local highway or landmark sentence appear.
